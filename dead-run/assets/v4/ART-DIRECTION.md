# Dodge roll and blood effects

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. The roll sheet uses `../sprites-source.png` as a survivor identity/style reference. Cropped runtime sprites and original PNG outputs are saved in this directory; `../../prepare-art-v4.cjs` is the reproducible Sharp extraction helper. Frame scale and ground baseline are shared.

## roll

Use case: stylized-concept. A production 16-bit animation sprite sheet matching the rear-view survivor in the reference: olive jacket, rust-red backpack, dark hair, brown cargo trousers and boots. EXACTLY EIGHT frames in a 4 column x 2 row even grid on transparent background. A FORWARD DODGE ROLL away from the viewer, going under a low obstacle. Sequence in reading order: 1 crouch and lean forward, 2 tuck head and shoulders low, 3 shoulders touch ground with backpack rising, 4 tightly curled body somersault with boot soles above back, 5 roll across upper back with knees tucked, 6 feet swing down and land in low squat, 7 rising from crouch, 8 resume rear-view running. This is a real complete somersault, not a slide, not eight standing figures. Keep same scale and shared ground baseline. The tucked poses should be much lower and rounder than standing frame8. Weapon secured against chest, mostly occluded, no loose gun. Sharp chunky low-resolution pixel clusters, charcoal olive rust bone palette. Generous padding each cell, no text no background no scene no shadows. Preserve survivor identity and clothes.

## blood

Use case: stylized-concept. Production 16-bit zombie game effects sprite atlas on transparent background. EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows equal cells with generous padding and no labels. Rich dark crimson and oxblood red, brighter red highlights, sharp deliberate pixel clusters matching gritty olive/rust 16-bit game. Row1 four consecutive animation frames of an EXPLODING BLOOD BURST: tight bright red impact, large violent radial crimson splash with many thick droplets, expanding ragged red cloud and droplets, dissipating sparse droplets. Clearly blood, no orange fire, no smoke, no realistic organs or body parts. Row2 cell1 a top-down ragged dark red blood puddle; cell2 a top-down scattered irregular blood splatter; cell3 a perfectly rectangular horizontal texture of thick flowing crimson liquid with brighter red surface ripples for the fill of a blood XP bar (only liquid no border), cell4 three small suspended crimson drops for flying XP particles. Every asset separate. No text no scene no watermark. All cells true transparent background except the liquid rectangle.
