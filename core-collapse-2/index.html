How to play

◎

Aim & release

Move around the star to aim. Click (or tap, or Space) to release the next element toward the core. The dotted path shows where it lands.

⚛

Fuse

Two identical elements fuse into the next: H → He → C → O → Ne → Mg → Si → Fe. Identical elements that nearly touch are drawn together by fusion affinity. Fuse in quick succession to build a chain multiplier.

⬢

Feed the core

Iron can't fuse. Instead it sinks into the core. Feed the core enough iron and the star goes supernova, and you move on to the next star.

⚠

Stay inside

Anything that lingers past the glowing danger ring starts a countdown. If one runs out, the star sheds its envelope: mass ejection, run over.

⇄

Hold

Right-click, C or the HOLD button stashes the current element for later (once per release).

☀

Solar Flare

Fusions charge the flare. Unleash it (F) to vaporize every H and He in the star, clearing room fast.

✧

Strange matter

Neutron shards upgrade whatever they touch. Dark matter is inert; three fusions next to it dissolve it.

✦

Boons

After each supernova, pick a boon. Each new star brings new hazards: magnetic swirl, binary companions and stellar flares.
