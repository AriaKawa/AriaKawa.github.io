Victory

How to play

Buy a building. Choose a hex. Battle when ready.

Upgrades
Buy a matching shop building to add a star, up to three.
Unit buildings
Send their units into each battle.
Support buildings
Boost unit buildings on adjacent hexes.
Economy buildings
Earn gold at the end of each round.
Interest
+1 gold per 10 saved, up to +5.
Streaks
2 / 4 / 5 wins or losses earn +1 / +2 / +3.
Level up
More buildings and rarer shop cards.
Win
Reach 10 victories before health runs out.

Saved armies fight asynchronously. When no army is available, you face a practice village. Your run saves automatically.
