    How to play

    Collect energy to grow your laser trail.

    Hitting any trail, including your own, crashes your bike. Avoid platforms and the arena boundary.

    Jump lifts your bike and laser trail, leaving an arch riders can pass underneath. The arch stays until that section of trail expires.

    90° uses instant right-angle turns with no reversing. 360° allows free steering. Boost spends trail length.

    SteerMouse / WASD / arrowsBoostHold Shift / left clickJumpSpace / right clickPauseEsc / P
