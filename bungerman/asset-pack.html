Asset pack requirements

The arcade edition includes painted animation sheets for all four characters and a generated cabinet. Each logical asset is a distinct original image. Directional walks and throws use actual illustrated frames.

Download the specification (JSON)

Style briefs

  • arcade2005 — Rounded, shaded 2005 handheld arcade characters with hand-painted highlights, depth and directional animation. Green inset arena, silver pillars, chunky brick, illustrated arcade cabinet.

Production contract

  1. One image generation call per logical asset. Animation sheets count as one asset per character. Never reuse another character’s sheet. Preserve originals unchanged; source rectangles are selected at runtime.
  2. Save exact prompts under asset-prompts/<style> and original output PNGs unchanged under dist/assets/<style>.
  3. Peanut: anthropomorphic peanut, propeller cap, white gloves and shoes.
  4. HutchMF: human, extremely heavyset with large belly, backwards black cap, beard, black polo, jeans, tattoo arms.
  5. Ninja: slender human, spiky blue hair, yellow headband, blue hoodie.
  6. Use consistent perspective, lighting, palette, silhouette and outline weights within each pack.
  7. UI buttons, borders, indicators and accessibility labels are code-native controls themed with each pack, not images of text.
  8. Assets load atomically; never mix styles or silently substitute old assets. Disable Play during load and show a retryable error.
  9. Original images are preserved. Runtime alpha-bounds cropping only removes transparent padding for consistent display.
  10. Validate unique hashes and complete inventory, inspect each style in character selection, gameplay and menus at desktop and mobile sizes.
  11. Environment animation: accelerating bomb pulse, placement bounce, burning fuse sparks, connected flame ignition and decay, brick debris, pickup rotation, warned bomb-rain descent.
  12. Pause must freeze gameplay animation and timers. Reduced motion must suppress cosmetic shake, bob and particles while keeping gameplay states readable.
  13. T-Pain: Black human, beard and locs, sunglasses, hat, gold chain, purple jacket. Equal gameplay polish to the other three characters.
  14. Use illustrated frames for all four characters in the arena, menu and HUD. Runtime may mirror side frames, but may not substitute geometric character rigs.
  15. Each sheet: 12 front-walk frames, 12 right-walk, 12 back-walk, 12 throwing poses, 6 front standing idles, 3 side standing idles and 3 back standing idles. Feet remain grounded during idle.
  16. Tap to plant; hold and release to throw along a clear corridor. Bombs travel on visible arcs, reserve landing cells, and start their fuse on landing.
  17. AI uses reaction delays, persistent goals, personalities, and timed escape planning. No direct input reading or instant perfect reactions.
  18. Gait advances once per 3.125 travelled tiles, not once per tile. Visual movement uses constant-speed travel; idle uses a dedicated relaxed standing loop.
  19. Throw animation lasts 0.36 seconds with the projectile leaving the hand at 0.18 seconds. Pause freezes animation; reduced motion freezes idle cosmetics.
  20. Dedicated 12-frame side-walk sheets must include feet-together passing positions and opposing contacts; repeated spread-leg stances do not count as a complete walk.
  21. Charging fills twice as fast and clamps at full power indefinitely. A held bomb never overcooks; only released bombs start their fuse after landing.
  22. HUD is a separate row above the arena with larger hearts, timer, sound, then pause. No heat meter, bomb-stock dots, Home button, control legend or Fan Demo footer.
  23. Painted cabinet joystick and gold buttons act as touch controls, with no separate visible control bar. Player damage produces a red edge pulse, sprite highlight, lost-heart floater and enlarged red hearts.

Required path: dist/assets/<style>/<asset>.png. Use square canvases for game objects, full-body portraits for the cast and a wide canvas for the wordmark. Preserve original alpha. Runtime trims transparent padding and scales to the shared game grid. Generated source resolution must be at least 128 pixels on each side.

Acceptance checks

Run node validate-packs.mjs for complete inventory, PNG headers, minimum dimensions, and unique file hashes. Run npm test for shared game mechanics. Inspect all images for consistent style, transparent edges, legible silhouettes and correct character identities. Play each style on desktop and mobile; verify all four menu states and atomic style loading.

Bomb rain

Begins with 25 seconds left. Two marked drops every 2.5 seconds; increases to three every 1.1 seconds during the final 10 seconds. Landing markers last 1.2 seconds, followed by a 1.35-second fuse. Drops use empty floor cells, regular blast rules and chain reactions. Bots see the danger too. Pending drops reset between rounds.
