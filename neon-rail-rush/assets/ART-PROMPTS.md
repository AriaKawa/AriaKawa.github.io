Create a new transparent pixel-art sprite strip with exactly two isolated rear-view sprites: a jumping pose with legs lifted and bent and arms out for balance; and a naturally low sliding/crouching pose with torso folded, one leg forward and one knee bent. Same purple hair, turquoise diamond-back jacket, coral scarf, navy trousers and white/coral sneakers. Same character scale, with crouching height about half standing height. Crisp 16-bit SNES pixel art with dark outlines, opaque sprites and transparent background. No glows, shadows, other objects, text or floor.

Original artwork generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Game-ready files are cropped/resized from these generated sources, preserving alpha transparency. Source sprites: four run poses, subway train, jump barricade, slide gate, coin. Other scenery, track tiles, particles and pickup symbols are generated in the renderer; music and effects are synthesized with Web Audio.
